Bloom Faces - Privacy Policy

Bloom Faces is a watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ).

Bloom Faces does not collect, store or transmit health data, location or personal information. Heart rate, Body Battery, steps, calories, stress, floors, active minutes, breathing, recovery time, battery and weather are read on the watch from Garmin's own system data and are only used to draw the face. Nothing leaves your watch. There are no accounts, analytics or advertising, and the app does not use the internet.

When you tap a field on the face, Bloom Faces asks the watch to open the matching Garmin screen (for example heart rate or weather). This is done by the watch itself and shares no data with Bloom Faces or with anyone else.

Bloom Faces is bought once through the Garmin Connect IQ Store; the payment is handled by Garmin and Bloom Faces does not see it.
