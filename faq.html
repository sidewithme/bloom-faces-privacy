Bloom Faces - FAQ and contact

Bloom Faces is a floral watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ): a peony that opens each time you raise your wrist. It is free to try; one payment through KiezelPay unlocks it for good. More flowers will come in later updates.

How does the trial and the payment work?

The free trial starts when you install the watch face. When it ends, the watch shows a code and the address kzl.io. Enter the code there to pay once; the watch then stays unlocked for good.

I paid but the watch still shows the code

Keep the watch connected to your phone. The watch checks the purchase about every 5 minutes, so it can take a few minutes to unlock. If it still shows the code after 15 minutes, restart the watch face (switch to another one and back) and wait a little longer.

Which watches are supported?

Round AMOLED Garmin watches listed on the Connect IQ Store page of the app. Watches with the older MIP screens are not supported because the flower photos do not look good on them.

What does the watch face show?

The time and the date, the weather at the top (current temperature, today's low and high, chance of rain), and six values: heart rate, Body Battery, steps, calories, watch battery and the battery days left. Steps, calories and battery have a small ring that fills up as you get closer to the goal.

Why does the flower open only sometimes?

The opening plays once each time the watch wakes up, for example when you raise your wrist, and then the open flower stays. Garmin watches do not allow a watch face to animate all the time, to save battery.

Does it work with the always-on display?

Yes. When the screen dims, the watch face switches to a simple black screen with only the time and the date, and moves it slightly every minute to protect the AMOLED screen. Turn always-on on or off in the watch's own display settings.

The weather shows dashes

The weather comes from your watch, which gets it from Garmin Connect on your phone. Keep the watch connected to the phone and the values appear after the next weather update.

Refunds

The payment is handled by KiezelPay (kiezelpay.com), not by Garmin, so Garmin cannot issue refunds for it. Please write to the contact below and include the code shown on your watch.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: no health data or location leaves your watch. To check the trial and purchase status, the watch sends KiezelPay its unique device identifier and model number.
