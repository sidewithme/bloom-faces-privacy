Bloom Faces - FAQ and contact

Bloom Faces is a floral watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ): a flower that opens each time you raise your wrist. There are five flowers (poppy, peony, snowdrop, crocus and sunflower) and you choose what the six fields show. It is bought once in the Garmin Connect IQ Store and everything is included.

How do I change the flower or the fields?

In Garmin Connect on your phone, open the watch face settings. Or on the watch: press and hold the MENU button (or touch and hold the screen), then Watch Face > Customize. Pressing and holding on the flower itself opens or closes it instead.

What happens when I tap a field?

On watches with Connect IQ 4.2 or newer, tapping a field opens the matching Garmin screen: heart rate, Body Battery, steps, weather and so on. On older watches nothing happens. The watch itself opens the screen; Bloom Faces receives no data.

Which watches are supported?

Round AMOLED Garmin watches listed on the Connect IQ Store page of the app. Watches with the older MIP screens are not supported because the flower photos do not look good on them.

What does the watch face show?

The time and the date, the watch battery at the top (green from 50%, red below) and six fields in two columns. On the left: temperature, low and high, chance of rain, feels like, humidity, wind or UV index. On the right: steps (top field only), heart rate, Body Battery, calories, stress, floors, active minutes, breaths per minute, recovery time or watch battery. Temperature is always in Celsius.

Why does the flower open only sometimes?

The opening plays once each time the watch wakes up, for example when you raise your wrist, and then the open flower stays. Garmin watches do not allow a watch face to animate all the time, to save battery.

Does it work with the always-on display?

Yes. When the screen dims, the watch face switches to a simple black screen with the time, the date and your values, and moves them slightly every minute to protect the AMOLED screen. Turn always-on on or off in the watch's own display settings.

The weather shows dashes

The weather comes from your watch, which gets it from Garmin Connect on your phone. Keep the watch connected to the phone and the values appear after the next weather update.

Refunds and purchase problems

The purchase is made in the Garmin Connect IQ Store, so refunds and purchase questions go through Garmin. For anything about the watch face itself, write to the contact below.

Privacy
